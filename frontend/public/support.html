Frequently asked questions

  • Where is my data stored? Everything you log stays on your device. Luna has no accounts and never uploads your health data.
  • How do the AI insights work? They're generated entirely on your device from the data you log — no internet or third-party AI is used.
  • How do I delete my data? Go to Settings → Data Management. You can clear any category or wipe everything. Deleting the app also removes all data.
  • Is the community really anonymous? Yes. Only the anonymous name, colour, and text you choose to post are shared — never your health data. You can report content or block users anytime.
  • Is Luna a medical service? No. Luna is a wellness and self-tracking tool and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider.
