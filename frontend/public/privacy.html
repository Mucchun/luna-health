🌸 Luna Health — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Luna Health is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what we do and do not collect.

What we store

  • All of your health information — symptoms, cycles, triggers, lab values, medications, and profile — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any third-party server.
  • We do not use any external AI service. All insights and chat responses are generated on your device.
  • There are no accounts and no login. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

Analytics & tracking

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Anonymous community

  • If you choose to post in the Community, only the anonymous content you share (a made-up display name, a colour, and your post text) is sent to the shared community server.
  • Your personal health data is never attached to a community post.
  • You can delete your own posts and replies, report content, or block users at any time.

Your control

  • You can delete any category of data, or wipe everything, from Settings at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes all on-device data.

Children

Luna Health is not directed to children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Luna Health is a wellness and self-tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions.
